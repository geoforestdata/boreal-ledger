Forestry Question

The workflow separates evidence before it interprets it.

The analysis deliberately keeps dated canopy loss, disturbance attribution, spectral recovery, forest structure, and carbon product uncertainty as separate claims. That separation matters because the remote-sensing signals are useful, but not interchangeable.

Canopy height is not stand age.

NBR is not biomass recovery.

Non-fire Hansen loss is not directly observed harvest.

Recovery

A probable post-harvest spectral recovery chronosequence.

Sentinel-2 NBR from 2024 is grouped by years since Hansen canopy loss. The >=100 pixel filter retained 13 of 24 age classes, making the curve a filtered spatial chronosequence rather than a complete time series.

NBR is a spectral vegetation recovery signal. It is not biomass recovery, structural recovery, or stand age.

Carbon

Carbon is the strongest uncertainty signal in the project.

ESA CCI, SCANFI, and MRNF do not converge to a single AGB carbon number. The disagreement is not a footnote; it is the analytical result that determines how defensible the carbon story can be.

Uncertainty / Validation

Common footprint, provincial reference, cautious conclusion.

The MRNF productive-stand footprint covers 2,489.6 ha and 431 stands. On that shared area, SCANFI had lower area-weighted RMSE than ESA CCI relative to MRNF.

For this study area, SCANFI showed better agreement with the Quebec provincial inventory-derived reference than ESA CCI on a common spatial footprint.

MRNF is not ground truth. It is a local, inventory-derived reference used to structure product uncertainty.
